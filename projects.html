Academic Research

Regulatory Evaluation of 'Sugar-Free' Nutrient Claims on Dairy-Based Products

DIPSAR / DPSRU 2023 - 2025

Objective

To evaluate the labeling compliance and claim accuracy of commercially available sugar-free ice creams (Amul & Mother Dairy Dietz) according to FSSAI Packaging, Labelling, and Advertising Regulations.

Methodology

Conducted a real-time label analysis and comparative evaluation of the products. Key parameters checked included ingredient lists, nutritional information (total vs. added sugar), mandatory warnings, and FSSAI license details.

Results & Conclusion

The study revealed that while both products claimed to be "sugar-free" and declared "0g added sugar," they contained natural sugars (lactose) exceeding the FSSAI limit of 0.5g/100g. The project highlighted the need for clearer differentiation between added and total sugars, and stricter warnings for diabetic consumers.



Published Review Paper

Recent Advancements in Heterocyclic Scaffolds as Potential Anti-cancer Agents Targeting EGFR and VEGFR-2

Journal: BMS-MRMC Published: Jan 2026

Objective

To review and compile recent advancements in the development of heterocyclic scaffolds (such as pyridine, pyrimidine, quinazoline, and triazole derivatives) that target EGFR and VEGFR-2 receptors for cancer therapy.

Methodology

Analyzed extensive in-vitro studies, structure-activity relationships (SAR), pharmacokinetic properties, and toxicity profiles of various novel compounds. Focused on dual inhibitors that can overcome drug resistance seen in standard mono-therapies.

Results & Conclusion

The review identified several promising heterocyclic compounds that demonstrated potent anti-cancer activity and significant cytotoxicity against various cancer cell lines. It concluded that multi-targeted approaches (inhibiting both EGFR and VEGFR-2) offer substantial prospects for developing next-generation anticancer therapeutics.
